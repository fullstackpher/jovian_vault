## 🔗 关联笔记

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  link(file.link, title) as "笔记",
  type,
  status
FROM ""
WHERE contains(file.tags, "evergreen")
  AND file.name != this.file.name
  AND (contains(area, this.area) OR contains(this.related, "[[" + file.name + "]]"))
SORT file.name ASC
LIMIT 10
```
